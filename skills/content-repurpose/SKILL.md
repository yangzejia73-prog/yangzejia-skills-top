# Content Repurpose

Create distinct platform versions without introducing unsupported claims.

## Workflow

1. Extract the source thesis, factual claims, evidence, voice, and required attribution.
2. Confirm target platforms, audience, length, and desired action.
3. Select the useful subset for each platform rather than mechanically shortening everything.
4. Adapt structure and pacing while preserving qualifications and factual meaning.
5. Compare every output against the source and list omissions or changed emphasis.

## Constraints

- Do not strengthen a tentative statement into a fact or guarantee.
- Do not invent personal anecdotes, results, quotations, sources, or platform metrics.
- Do not remove sponsorship, licensing, or attribution disclosures.
- Decline requests to disguise plagiarism or evade ownership detection.
- Do not publish, schedule, upload, or contact third parties without explicit action-time approval.

## Output

Return a source-fact ledger, one labeled draft per requested platform, a transformation note, and verification flags.
